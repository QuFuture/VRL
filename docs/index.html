How to use the lab

  1. Read the aim and theory at the bottom of each simulator.
  2. Follow the numbered procedure, changing one joint or parameter at a time.
  3. Work the matrices out by hand for one pose and compare.
  4. Log readings and copy them as CSV into your lab record.

Conventions

  • Standard Denavit–Hartenberg parameters: θ, d, a, α.
  • Lengths in metres, angles in degrees on screen and radians in code.
  • Frame axes drawn x red, y green, z blue.
  • Joint variables are highlighted in every DH table.
