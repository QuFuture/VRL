Inverse kinematics

The planar part is a two-link arm. The law of cosines gives the elbow angle:

c₂ = (x² + y² − a₁² − a₂²) / (2a₁a₂) θ₂ = ± acos(c₂) θ₁ = atan2(y, x) − atan2(a₂ sin θ₂, a₁ + a₂ cos θ₂)

The vertical and wrist joints decouple:

d₃ = d₁ − d₄ − z θ₄ = θ₁ + θ₂ − φ

Procedure

  1. Press Right side. Record both solutions and check that each one puts the tool on the target.
  2. Change only z. Confirm that only d₃ changes.
  3. Change only φ. Confirm that only θ₄ changes, and explain the sign in θ₄ = θ₁ + θ₂ − φ.
  4. Press Full reach. Explain why both solutions become the same (θ₂ = 0).
  5. Press Behind left and find which shape breaks a joint limit.
  6. Trace the circle with each arm shape. Note where, if anywhere, the chosen shape cannot follow the path.

Things to notice

  • If |c₂| > 1 the point is farther than a₁ + a₂ (or closer than |a₁ − a₂|), so no solution exists.
  • At |c₂| = 1 the two shapes merge. This is the boundary singularity from Experiment 5.
  • atan2 gives the correct quadrant automatically. Plain atan would give wrong answers behind the robot.
  • θ₄ is wrapped into ±180°, since a full turn of the wrist gives the same tool angle.
