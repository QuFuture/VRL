Equations of motion

With angles measured from the horizontal, centre of mass at mid-link (l꜀ = l/2) and I = ml²/12:

M₁₁ = I₁ + I₂ + m₁l꜀₁² + m₂(l₁² + l꜀₂² + 2l₁l꜀₂c₂) M₁₂ = I₂ + m₂(l꜀₂² + l₁l꜀₂c₂) M₂₂ = I₂ + m₂l꜀₂² h = m₂l₁l꜀₂ s₂ Cq̇ = [ −h(2θ̇₁θ̇₂ + θ̇₂²) , h θ̇₁² ]ᵀ G₁ = (m₁l꜀₁ + m₂l₁) g c₁ + m₂l꜀₂ g c₁₂ G₂ = m₂l꜀₂ g c₁₂

Procedure

  1. Choose Free swing with damping b = 0. Check that the total energy stays constant. This verifies the model and integrator.
  2. Restart and watch the double pendulum. Change the start pose by 1° and compare. Note the sensitivity to initial conditions.
  3. Choose PD only. Record the steady-state error and explain it using G(q).
  4. Choose PD + gravity. Show that the error goes to zero for the same gains.
  5. Choose Computed torque with Kₚ = ωₙ², K_d = 2ζωₙ. Check that both joints respond like the same second-order system.
  6. Increase m₂ (a heavier payload) without changing the controller and compare the four responses.

How it is computed

  • Each step solves q̈ = M(q)⁻¹(τ − Cq̇ − G − b q̇) and integrates with fourth-order Runge–Kutta at 1 ms.
  • The controller runs inside every integration stage, as a continuous-time controller would.
  • For PD control, Kₚ and K_d are in N·m/rad and N·m·s/rad. For computed torque they act on acceleration (1/s² and 1/s), because M(q) scales the output.
  • Energy E = ½q̇ᵀMq̇ + m₁g y꜀₁ + m₂g y꜀₂. Any drift with τ = 0 and b = 0 comes from numerical error.
All parameters are editable. Defaults approximate a light educational arm.
